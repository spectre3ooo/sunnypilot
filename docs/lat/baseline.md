# Phase 0 baseline — Ioniq 5 low-speed lateral limits

Generated by `openpilot/tools/lat_metrics/lat_metrics.py` over 25 rlog segments
(8 routes, Johnson City / Elizabethton TN, 3–6 Sep 2026), branch
`ioniq5-lowspeed-lateral`, stock limits throughout.

All figures are **pooled** over segments. A frame-weighted mean of per-segment
p95 values is not the p95 of the combined sample, and a rate share must be
weighted by limited frames rather than by total frames.

Companion: `ioniq5-lateral-improvement-plan.md` §2, §5, §8.

## Headline: the rate limit binds, the cap does not

| Speed band | frames | limited | cap-limited | rate-limited | rate share of limited |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| < 15 mph | 25,374 | 0.807 | **0.004** | **0.803** | 0.99 |
| 15–25 mph | 41,276 | 0.730 | **0.001** | **0.729** | 1.00 |
| 25–40 mph | 63,003 | 0.535 | **0.001** | **0.534** | 1.00 |
| > 40 mph | 8,395 | 0.436 | **0.000** | **0.436** | 1.00 |
| all | 138,048 | 0.637 | 0.002 | 0.636 | 1.00 |

*limited* = frames where `|carControl.actuators.torque − carOutput.actuatorsOutput.torque| > 0.01`
with lateral control active. *cap-limited* = of those, `|carOutput torque| ≥ 0.98`.

Below 15 mph the Python limiter alters the controller's request in **80.7 %** of
engaged frames, and **99 %** of that is the rate limit. The 270-count cap binds in
**0.1–0.4 %** of frames at every speed. Torque saturation is ≤ 0.8 % everywhere and
0.00 % below 25 mph.

This settles the Phase 0 question and matches plan §2: raising `STEER_MAX` without
raising `STEER_DELTA_UP/DOWN` would move a limit that is almost never active. The
Phase 2 envelope must lead with the rate.

## Tracking error grows as speed falls

| Speed band | pooled p95 \|desired − actual\| lat accel (m/s²) | peak error, second bend (m/s²) |
|---|---|---|
| < 15 mph | 0.733 | 1.619 |
| 15–25 mph | 0.579 | 1.072 |
| 25–40 mph | 0.346 | 0.716 |
| > 40 mph | 0.221 | 0.119 |

The second-bend peak — error immediately after a torque reversal, where the rate
limit has just spent its budget crossing zero — is **13× larger below 15 mph than
above 40**. That is "running wide on the exit of an S-turn", quantified.

## Reversal times

Median time from a `carOutput` torque peak to the next opposite-sign peak, counting
only pairs whose intervening frames are also in the band:

| Speed band | reversals | median (s) |
|---|---|---|
| < 15 mph | 19 | 3.18 |
| 15–25 mph | 29 | 2.47 |
| 25–40 mph | 26 | 4.83 |
| > 40 mph | 1 | 10.06 |

**Caveat:** this is peak-to-peak as the plan specifies, so it still includes dwell
time in each bend, not only the rate-limited transit between them. It is a valid
before/after comparator but must not be read directly against plan §2's 2.25 s
figure for a full ±270 swing. Isolating the transition (first departure from a peak
to first arrival at the opposite peak) would be the sharper metric and is worth
adding before Phase 2 gates on it. The `> 40 mph` figure rests on a single
reversal and should be ignored.

## Driver interventions

| Speed band | frames | `steeringPressed` while `latActive` | share |
|---|---|---|---|
| < 15 mph | 25,374 | 12,918 | **50.9 %** |
| 15–25 mph | 41,276 | 13,624 | **33.0 %** |
| 25–40 mph | 63,003 | 7,527 | 11.9 % |
| > 40 mph | 8,395 | 1,662 | 19.8 % |

Hands are on the wheel against the system for **half of all sub-15 mph engaged
driving**. Plan §8.2 calls interventions "the metric that matters most"; this is the
baseline they must be measured against.

`steerFaultTemporary`: **0 events** across all 25 segments. The fault-avoidance cut
is doing its job — the cost is the thump, not a fault.

## Steer-request cuts (plan §5)

- **130 cut events**, **130/130** at `|steeringAngleDeg| ≥ 85` — the mechanism fires
  exactly where the source says it does.
- **Every cut is exactly 2 frames** (`MAX_ANGLE_CONSECUTIVE_FRAMES = 2`), spacing
  median 91 frames (= `MAX_ANGLE_FRAMES` 89 + 2).
- Torque value held non-zero through the cut in **113/130**; in the remaining 17 the
  request was already at or near zero.
- `steeringTorqueEps` swings a median of **4.8** counts (max 28.4) within 150 ms of
  each cut. That is the once-a-second thump, measured.

**Methodological note.** A first pass reported 140 cuts with 4 below 85°. All four
were lateral *disengagements*: `latActive` goes false and drops `ActToiSta` as a side
effect. Counting every falling edge conflates disengagement with fault avoidance,
which would inflate cut counts precisely in the tight corners where both occur. The
metric now requires lateral control to remain active across the drop.

## Inside-cutting clearance

Minimum clearance from `modelV2.position` to the inner lane line — or road edge where
no confident line exists — over x ∈ [0, 30] m, per curve frame
(`|desiredCurvature| > 0.005`), pooled:

| Speed band | curve frames | median | p5 | worst | frames crossing | road edge used |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| < 15 mph | 22 | +0.96 m | +0.32 m | +0.21 m | 0 | 27 % |
| 15–25 mph | 323 | +1.01 m | +0.53 m | +0.30 m | 0 | 1 % |
| 25–40 mph | 1,505 | +0.93 m | +0.52 m | **−0.19 m** | 1 | 20 % |

**There is no clear speed trend** — medians are flat at 0.93–1.01 m. An earlier
version of this report claimed clearance shrinks as speed falls; that was an
artifact of the band-selection bug described below, and is retracted.

Exactly **one** curve frame crosses the boundary, at 25–40 mph. Curve frames below
15 mph are scarce (22), so the sub-15 row is weak evidence either way.

Phase 3's default margins (1.05 m to a line, 1.3 m to an edge) sit **at or above the
median measured clearance** of ~0.95 m. Corridor keeping as specified would therefore
be active a substantial fraction of curve time on these roads, rather than the
"identically zero except near the margin" behaviour plan §11.6 anticipates. The
margins are worth revisiting against this data before Phase 3 — but note this is an
argument about how often it engages, not evidence that the car is cutting: it very
nearly never does here.

On these rural roads lane lines are frequently absent (`laneLineProbs` ≈ 0.0 for long
stretches) and road edges often exceed the 0.3 std gate, so many curve frames yield no
usable boundary at all and are excluded entirely. Any Phase 3 validation must report
boundary availability alongside clearance, or it will silently measure only the frames
where detection happened to work.

## Corrections applied to this report

An adversarial review of `lat_metrics.py` found three defects that had produced
plausible but wrong numbers in the first version. All are fixed; the first two are
pinned by regression tests in `test_lat_metrics.py`.

1. **Clearance was selected by time range, not band membership.** Model frames were
   taken from the convex hull of each speed band (`mt` between `t[band].min()` and
   `t[band].max()`). Speed bands are not contiguous, so a low clearance recorded at
   30 mph was also reported as the worst clearance of the <15 mph band — visible in
   the old table as an identical −0.19 m "worst" in all three sub-40 rows. It also
   dropped the `latActive` requirement.
2. **Reversals were paired across out-of-band gaps.** A bend before a gap could pair
   with a bend after it, counting the intervening out-of-band time as reversal
   duration. Median reversal times fell substantially once fixed (15–25 mph: 6.29 s →
   2.47 s).
3. **Aggregates averaged per-segment percentiles.** The summary table took a
   frame-weighted mean of per-segment p95 values, which is not the pooled p95, and
   weighted the rate share by total frames rather than limited frames. All summary
   figures are now pooled from raw samples.

**The headline cap-vs-rate result was unaffected by all three** — it indexes by the
per-frame band mask throughout — as were the cut analysis and intervention shares.

## Caveats

- `liveTorqueParameters` and `livePose` are **absent** from these rlogs, contrary to
  plan §7. The §8.2 lateral-jerk comfort metric will need raw `accelerometer` instead.
- `--geofence` is accepted but not yet applied; per-segment statistics stand without it.
- Segments are whole 60 s recordings, not hand-picked corners. Fixed segments per
  plan §8.1 still need to be chosen and geofenced.
